## Order of work

1. **The detector** (`forskscope-compare`):
   - §1's list in one place, with a test that holds it equal to Unicode's
     `Bidi_Control` property;
   - a pure function giving each side's count.
   
   **Say where the `Bidi_Control` truth comes from:**
   - a crate already in the tree;
   - or a table checked against the Unicode data file's published values,
     written into the test.
   
   **No new runtime dependency** without asking.
2. **The view** (§2 and §3):
   - the marker in place of the character;
   - character-level highlighting when the marker is the change;
   - `unicode-bidi: isolate` on each line's text cell;
   - **the merge view too.**
   - Copying copies the marker (§2).
3. **The notices** (§4):
   - the status bar's words;
   - the HTML report;
   - the CLI's escaping and notice;
   - the additive `bidi_controls` field in the JSON and in MCP's
     `compare`;
   - the schema, and `cargo xtask json-schema`.
4. **What is saved** (§5): the byte-for-byte round trip, through *Use*,
   merge, save and patch export.
